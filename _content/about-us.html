About First Perfumes

Founded In 2006, On A Single Production Line

Welcome to First Perfumes, where exquisite fragrances meet unmatched sophistication. Established in 2006, First Perfumes has emerged as a cornerstone of the perfume manufacturing landscape in the United Arab Emirates. With a dedicated focus on providing premium quality perfume manufacturing services, we have steadily built a reputation for excellence that extends far beyond our borders.

Over the years, our dedication to excellence has propelled us to the forefront of the industry, and our influence has extended to international markets. Our fragrances have become sought-after not only within the UAE but also in prominent markets like the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Nigeria, and Kenya.
