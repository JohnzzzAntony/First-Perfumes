A Cosmetics Unit Kept Separate From The Perfume Hall

Our personal care lines run in their own air-handled suite at Jebel Ali Industrial First, physically separated from the alcohol-based fragrance compounding hall. Vacuum emulsifiers, jacketed mixing vessels and semi-automatic piston fillers let a formula move from a 50-litre pilot batch to a 2,000-litre production run without being rebuilt — the same base, the same order of addition, the same released specification.

From Brief To Released Batch

Every personal care project follows the same five stages, so you always know which document is due next and who signs it off.

  • Brief and benchmark — target texture, claims, pH window, pack format and destination markets.
  • Bench formulation — emulsion or anhydrous base built in the R&D lab, with fragrance dosed into that exact base.
  • Pilot batch — a 50-litre trial decanted for stability, packaging compatibility and preservative efficacy testing.
  • Documentation — ingredient declaration, safety assessment file and the notification pack for each market.
  • Scale-up — validated production batch, filled, labelled and released against the approved retained sample.

Ingredient Safety Is Settled Before The First Batch

Every raw material arrives with a specification sheet and a certificate of analysis, and is checked against the restricted and prohibited substance lists that apply in the market you are shipping to. Emulsifiers, preservatives and actives are screened for allergen declarations before they reach the bench, fragrance is dosed to an IFRA-conformant level for the product category, and each finished batch is held against a retained sample for the full shelf life.

Sustainable Sourcing

Ingredients We Can Trace. Packs We Can Recycle.

Sustainability in personal care is decided at the specification stage, not at the end of the line. We ask suppliers to document the origin of botanical extracts and carrier oils, prefer palm derivatives covered by a mass-balance chain of custody, and keep a shortlist of preservative systems that pass efficacy testing at lower use levels. On the pack side, our Jebel Ali assembly lines favour mono-material bottles and closures, unlaminated cartons and refill formats, and production water and solvent are recovered where the process allows. Where a claim cannot be substantiated with a supplier document, we do not print it.
