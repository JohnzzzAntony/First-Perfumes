About Us

Crafting Fragrances. Building Brands.

Founded in 2006 by visionary perfumer Mr. Abdullah Karji, First Perfumes Ind LLC has established itself as a leading name in the UAE's perfume and cosmetics manufacturing sector. In less than two decades, we have grown from a dedicated production facility into a globally recognised manufacturing unit, celebrated for precision, innovation, and craftsmanship. Whether it's creating heritage-inspired fragrances or cutting-edge modern formulations, we have always remained committed to excellence in manufacturing and innovation.

Process

From Brief to Finished Product

Our structured 8-step process ensures every product reaches market with confidence.

Let’s Work Together
STEP : 01

Briefing

We understand your client vision, market goals, target audience, and fragrance requirements in depth.

STEP : 02

R&D & Sampling

Our R&D lab develops formulations, selects raw materials, and shares prototypes for client approval.

STEP : 03

Production & Packaging

Batch, fill, and assemble under strict Quality Control checks. Bottles and boxes are designed and decorated in-house.

STEP : 04

Compliance & Delivery

Products are registered for global markets (UAE, EU, UK, USA) and shipped worldwide as finished, market-ready goods.
