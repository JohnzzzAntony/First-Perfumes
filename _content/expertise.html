How We Work

The Route Every Programme Takes

Start a Brief

Market, audience, price point, volume and launch date. Most programmes that go wrong go wrong here, because an unrealistic date was accepted rather than challenged.

Accords are built or matched, then tested for stability and compatibility against the actual base, alcohol grade and closure the product will ship with.

Bottle, closure and carton are selected, then soak-tested together. Components are only purchased once the combination is proven.

IFRA conformity, allergen declarations and market notifications for UAE, EU, UK and US are prepared alongside development, not after it.

The pilot runs in the same hall as the commercial batch, so maceration, filtration and settling behaviour are recorded before a production date is committed.

Quality control releases against the master batch record. The consignment is palletised, documented and booked.
