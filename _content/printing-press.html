Cartons, labels and boxes printed under the same roof

Our in-house printing press produces the secondary packaging that carries your brand: folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, labels, inserts and leaflets. Because print sits in the same Jebel Ali facility as filling and assembly, artwork, colour and die-lines are checked against the actual bottle and closure before a sheet is run.

Keeping print in-house shortens lead times, protects colour consistency between re-orders and removes the hand-off between a third-party printer and the packaging line. Short runs for launches and samples sit alongside full production volumes.

Print Process

From Artwork To Finished Packaging

Four stages, each signed off before the next begins.

Start a Brief
STEP : 01

Artwork and pre-press

We receive your artwork, check it for bleed, resolution, colour mode and barcode quality, and prepare the die-line to fit your bottle and insert. Any issues are flagged before plates are made.

STEP : 02

Proofing and approval

A printed proof is produced on the specified board with the chosen finishes, so colour and feel can be approved on the real material rather than on screen.

STEP : 03

Printing and finishing

The approved job is printed, then finished with foil, embossing, lamination or varnish as specified, and die-cut and creased to shape. Sheets are checked against the approved proof throughout the run.

STEP : 04

Assembly and delivery to the line

Boxes are glued, assembled and inspected, then moved directly to our packaging lines to be filled and packed with your product, or shipped on to you if you fill elsewhere.
