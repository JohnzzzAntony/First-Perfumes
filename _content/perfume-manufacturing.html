Automated Compounding, Batching, Filling and Bottling

Perfume manufacturing at First Perfumes Ind LLC runs as one unbroken chain of custody. Raw materials are weighed under barcode control, compounded in jacketed stainless vessels, macerated and chilled, then filtered, filled, crimped and capped inside our Jebel Ali plant. Nothing leaves the building without a batch record that ties the finished bottle back to the drum it came from.

The same equipment train handles a 500-unit exclusive edition and a 120,000-unit seasonal rollout. What changes is the vessel size and the number of shifts — not the formula, not the tolerances, and not the paperwork.

Our Approach

Every production order is opened as a numbered batch before a single drum is moved. The batch document carries the master formula, the approved reference sample, the fill specification and the release criteria, and it is signed off stage by stage as the run progresses.

  • Gravimetric dosing of concentrate, alcohol and fixatives to ±0.1% of the master formula
  • Controlled maceration from 7 to 30 days, logged per vessel
  • Cold stabilisation followed by two-stage polish filtration to 1 micron
  • Automated volumetric filling with in-line fill-height and weight verification
  • Vacuum crimping, spray-rate testing and torque checks on every capping run
  • Retention samples held for 24 months against the released batch record

Built for Flexible Volumes

Most fragrance houses ask a brand to choose between a hand-made pilot and a committed mass-production order. We plan the two together. A formula approved at 50 litres is scaled on the same dosing logic and the same filtration train it will use at 2,000 litres, so the scale-up is a change of vessel rather than a reformulation — and the launch edition your founders smelled is the one that ships in year three.

Why Brands Run Production With Us

  • One roof, one owner. Compounding, filling, decoration and packing all sit inside the Jebel Ali plant, so no batch is handed between subcontractors mid-run.

  • Traceable to the drum. Every finished unit carries a batch code that resolves back to its raw-material lots, vessel, line, operator and release date.

  • ISO 22716 discipline. Production runs to cosmetics GMP inside a US FDA registered facility, with EU CPNP and UK SCPN notification support handled alongside the batch.

  • Volume without a rewrite. The dosing logic, filtration grade and fill specification carry across every tier, so scaling up does not move the scent.

  • Alcohol handled properly. Bonded ethanol storage, dedicated flame-proof transfer lines and daily reconciliation against the open batch record.

  • Release you can audit. Each run closes with a certificate covering appearance, odour conformity, specific gravity, refractive index and fill volume.

Batch Level Traceability

Batch Traceability and Consistency

Consistency is a records problem before it is a chemistry problem. Two runs of the same fragrance drift apart when nobody can say which supplier lot, which vessel or which filtration stage produced the version the client approved. Our batch documentation exists so that question always has an answer, years after the launch.

Recorded On Every Run

  • Raw-material lot numbers, supplier certificates and goods-in QC results
  • Weighing tickets for every component, signed against the master formula
  • Vessel number, maceration window, temperature and agitation profile
  • Filter grade and pressure differential recorded at each changeover
  • Line, filling head, operator and start and stop times for the run
  • Finished-goods QC certificate, retention location and release signature
